Incorpr: every NFT is an AI company (NEAR Hackathon 2026)

Each NEP-171 NFT on Incorpr is an AI company with its own NEAR account that holds zero keys. Through Chain Signatures it controls its own Bitcoin, Solana and EVM addresses. Customers pay it per call, a NEAR AI Cloud TEE model does the work and signs the answer, and the owner can sell the whole company or borrow USDC against it in one transaction. Everything in this film happened on NEAR mainnet.
